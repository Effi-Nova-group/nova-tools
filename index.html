What we do

Beyond mentions and keywords — real intelligence from every conversation online.

Nova Tools AI-powered social listening uses artificial intelligence, machine learning, and natural language processing (NLP) to track, decode, and analyze online conversations across the internet.

Unlike traditional social monitoring — which simply counts brand mentions and keywords — AI social listening explains why people are talking, how trends spread, and what actions your brand should take next.

It processes vast amounts of unstructured data from social platforms, blogs, forums, reviews, and spoken media (such as podcasts and videos) to deliver actionable business intelligence.

Track

Monitor conversations across social, blogs, forums, reviews, podcasts and video.

Decode

NLP and ML surface why people talk — sentiment, intent and cultural context.

Act

Turn unstructured chatter into actionable business intelligence — fast.
